# Supplier Alert Review

## Customer

The first user is a trade-compliance analyst at a U.S. medical-supply importer or distributor. The first prospective buyer is the head of trade compliance. Validate the workflow by observing how an analyst connects a newly issued or amended notice to open purchase orders.

## Problem

A notice identifies a manufacturer, product, location, and effective date. A purchase order may identify only a distributor and a product description. The analyst has to verify the manufacturer, determine whether the notice applies to the order under review, and record the reason for following up.

## Product

A review queue connects the buyer's purchase orders to dated notices and supplier records. Each row shows the evidence behind its review status and the next information to request when a link is missing.

## What to measure

Give a reviewer three sample orders and ask which order needs supplier follow-up after the September determination. Record time to answer, whether they chose the right order, and whether they could locate the source behind the change.
